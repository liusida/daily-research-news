# What I care about
I am an independent researcher in interpretability and large models.

Score 3 (must see): mechanistic interpretability, sparse autoencoders / dictionary learning, circuits,
features & superposition, probing, steering / activation engineering, attribution, model internals,
"model biology" (what is going on inside LLMs), interpretability methods or tools.
Score 2 (likely useful): understanding LLM behavior or representations, training dynamics, emergent
abilities, scaling science, alignment work that studies model internals, evals that reveal how models think.
Score 1 (maybe): general LLM research with a small link to understanding models.
Score 0 (skip): applications, benchmarks for products, policy, community/events, unrelated ML.
