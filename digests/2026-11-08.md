# Research digest — 2026-11-08

## Top picks

### Does understanding models make them safer?
This survey asks whether studying model internals makes AI safer. It also proposes a way to judge claims about what happens inside models.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08438) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.08438" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 52</a>

### Finding features in a driving model
Researchers studied features inside a model that plans a car’s path. Changing individual features led to predictable changes in the path and braking.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41185) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.41185" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 10</a>

### Shaping and watching model internals
Researchers trained models to avoid using or expressing certain concepts. In some cases, a concept remained in the model in a form that a simple probe could miss.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07071) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.07071" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 14</a>

### Steering robot actions
Researchers changed a robot model’s internal action signals to help it keep track of objects. The model became more aware of objects and succeeded more often on robot tasks.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41009) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.41009" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 10</a>

### Teaching models facts they can use
Researchers studied why models can repeat new facts but struggle to reason with them. Their approach helped models use new facts to answer questions, such as who leads a person’s new country.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01519) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.01519" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 11</a>

### Removing distractions from medical questions
Researchers studied how irrelevant sentences lead medical models to wrong answers. Blocking the effect of a distracting sentence improved answers, though it also caused a small drop on questions without distractions.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35558) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.35558" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 10</a>

### Finding flaws in network weights
Researchers asked a language model to inspect the weights of small medical decision networks. It named flaws in most of the flawed networks, but found only one of three hidden triggers.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05341) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.05341" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 12</a>

### Watching for hidden misalignment
This post suggests looking at alignment test results across training for signs that a model may be hiding misalignment. In the author’s test, the expected rise and fall in misalignment scores did not appear.
[LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/iXKhtYuXtneXzw9fN/let-s-try-to-catch-misalignment-before-a-model-learns-to) <span class="likes" title="karma on LessWrong">▲ 5</span>
