## Top picks

### How easy are neurons to interpret?
A post discusses claims that transformer neurons are easier to interpret than previously thought. It cites work where removing most weak neuron activations caused only a small rise in error.

### Checking answers using model internals
A new approach checks answers using signals from inside a language model. It combines those signals with how likely the model considers its generated words.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07948) <a class="likes" href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.07948" title="likes on alphaXiv">♥ 10</a>

### Clean data can bring out hidden behavior
A study looks at how clean data can change the effect of poisoned training data. In small models, adding clean data led to hidden traits, such as speaking German, that the poisoned data alone did not teach.
[LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5CrmJfRY7yMaAoumC/homeopathic-learning-diluting-poisoned-fine-tuning-data-can-1) <span class="likes" title="karma on LessWrong">▲ 25</span>
