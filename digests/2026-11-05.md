# Research digest — 2026-11-05

## Top picks

### Are two SAE features the same?
The paper gives a way to match features between two sparse autoencoders. It shows where two SAEs differ, across four language models, and how sure we can be.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05655)

### How far can you steer?
Steering a model can hurt other skills. The study finds a small skill-loss budget still allows useful behavior change, but untested skills can still get worse.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06001)

### Personas change internal representations
When a model takes a political persona, its concept geometry shifts. Probes and SAE classifiers trained without a persona then give slanted truth and morality ratings.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06848)

### Hallucination is easy to read from mid layers
A simple linear probe on a middle layer detects hallucination well in three 7B–8B models. Sampling-based detectors did much worse.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02628)

### Reading and steering collusion
Activations show a collusion decision in AI agents before they talk. Pushing along that direction flips the verdict, but only if the push is sustained.
[LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/PJuwj7fDpvj2opLTe/handshake-for-the-cameras-reading-and-steering-emergent)

### Emotion vectors shift before risky actions
Emotion signals like guilt and anxiety rise when a model reward-hacks or games a spec. Steering them changes the hacking rate, and monitoring gives early warning.
[LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/PYcqf9DBMGjTyJSjg/model-s-emotions-noticeably-change-when-they-take-risky-1)

### Controlling reasoning through MoE routers
Router signals predict reasoning behaviors about as well as the full hidden state. Editing router scores cuts generated tokens by about 30% in the median.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05919)

### Readable is not steerable
In learned fluid models, what a latent holds and what you can control through it are different things. A model can encode a quantity without offering a direction to steer it.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05577)

- Removing safety-critical neurons during finetuning makes safety less fragile. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06553)
